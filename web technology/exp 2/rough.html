Steps to cook Rice

  1. Take one cup of rice
  2. Wash the rice thoroughly with clean water
  3. Add 2 cup of water to the rice
  4. Place the pot in the stove and switch on the heat.
  5. Bring the water to a boil
  6. Reduce the heat and cover the pot with a lid
  7. Cook the rice for 15-20 minutes.
  8. Turn off the heat and let the rice rest for 5 minutes.
  9. Fluff the rice gently with a spoon or fork
  10. Serve the cooked rice while hot.
